About

Passionate about
bringing change

📸
Upload photo

"I don't just study policy — I build the conditions where policy can actually work."

I am a BS International Relations student at the International Islamic University Islamabad (CGPA: 3.89/4.00), working at the intersection of research, advocacy, and on-the-ground coordination. What drives me is simple: I believe young people from Pakistan have a unique and necessary voice in global policy — and I am committed to making that voice count.

From co-developing climate workbooks for the Federal Ministry of Education to conducting archival research at the Army Institute of Military History, and engaging senior leadership at ISPR including research debates on current geopolitical topics — my experience bridges the academic and institutional worlds of policy work.

I founded Bright Path Alliance, delivered sessions to hundreds of students on climate change, career development, and mental health, and serve as a Board Member of the Global Shapers Community (World Economic Forum), where I lead youth programming on education equity and inclusion.

I also participated in the Kashmir Fellowship — writing essays and designing policy posters on the distinction between Kashmir and Azad Kashmir — and have been part of the Progressive Climate Foundation, documenting community engagement and plantation drives through articles and reports.
